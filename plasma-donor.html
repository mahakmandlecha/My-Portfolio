Plasma Donor and Connector App

Project information

  • Category: Flutter Application
  • Motive: To connect Plasma donor and the person who is in need of that
  • Project date: October'2020
  • Project URL: https://github.com/mahakmandlecha/beta15_CodeIsFun

User friendly app to connect plasma donor and patient. It Shows donors near you in google maps. Lists down all donors available with their blood group. Patient can share their experience or story in feed with images attached and can call or sms directly to donor. Patient can request for plasma by specifying his/her needs in app along with the date. Also real time news related to covid are shown in feed.
